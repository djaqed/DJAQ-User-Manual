Human-Compatible General-Purpose Computing Platform

Operating instructions for friendship, collaboration, problem-solving, shenanigans, creative projects, relationships, and extremely specific rabbit holes.

IMPORTANT: Maximum demonstrated capability should not be interpreted as minimum available capability.
01

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Djaq is a highly analytical, pattern-oriented, curiosity-driven system optimized for:

- understanding complex systems;
- identifying patterns and inconsistencies;
- acquiring enormous quantities of information about interesting subjects;
- solving problems once sufficiently engaged;
- direct and detailed communication;
- building tools, rules, and processes to compensate for unreliable automatic functions;
- forming deep attachments to people, interests, projects, and ideas.

Several processes that appear automatic on standard human hardware require conscious computation on Djaq.

This does not necessarily prevent successful execution.

It may, however, increase processing cost.

Core Principle

Capability and availability are independent variables.

A function being installed does not guarantee that the function can be called successfully at this exact moment.
02

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Djaq generally performs best under the following execution sequence:

"PURPOSE → STRUCTURE → RELEVANCE → ENGAGEMENT → MOMENTUM"

Once "MOMENTUM" has initialized, very large or complicated workloads may become surprisingly easy to process.

The most common failure occurs before "ENGAGEMENT".

Therefore:

«Difficulty starting a task does not reliably predict difficulty performing the task.»

A three-hour project may be easy.

Opening the file may be hard.

This is a known architecture feature.
03

TASK SCHEDULER

Preferred Task Format

For best results, provide:

"OBJECTIVE + REASON + CONSTRAINTS + DEADLINE"

Example:

«“Can you look at this tonight? I need to know whether these numbers match before I submit it tomorrow.”»

This provides all required metadata.

Less Reliable Format

«“Remember to look at this sometime.”»

This process may be immediately terminated by the operating system.

Important

Djaq's memory does not prioritize information solely according to its importance.

Therefore:

"IMPORTANT ≠ AUTOMATICALLY REMEMBERED"

Calendars, reminders, alarms, notes, checklists, and messages are external memory peripherals.

Their use should not be interpreted as evidence that the underlying subject is unimportant.
04

ATTENTION MANAGEMENT

Djaq does not have a simple quantity of “attention.”

Attention is dynamically allocated according to variables including:

- interest;
- novelty;
- urgency;
- immediate relevance;
- complexity;
- environmental stimulation;
- existing momentum.

Consequently, both of the following states are normal:

"STATE A: Research one subject continuously for 5 hours."

"STATE B: Read the same paragraph 4 times without retaining it."

These are not contradictory.

Troubleshooting

If attention is failing, increasing moral pressure is generally ineffective.

Try changing one of the actual variables:

- make the objective concrete;
- reduce distractions;
- create urgency;
- introduce novelty;
- break the task down;
- begin the first step together;
- explain why the task matters;
- make the next action immediately executable.

COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

Preferred Protocol

Djaq strongly prefers explicit communication.

If you:

- want something;
- dislike something;
- are upset;
- need space;
- want reassurance;
- are attracted to Djaq;
- are no longer attracted to Djaq;
- think a boundary has been crossed;
- want a relationship to change;

please transmit this information directly.

Do not assume Djaq will reliably infer it from hints, body language, tone changes, strategic silence, or behavior that “should make it obvious.”

The system is capable of sophisticated social reasoning.

The automatic INTENTION DETECTION service, however, is unreliable.

Recommended

«“When you did X, I felt Y, and I'd prefer Z.”»

Not Recommended

«“It's fine.”»

when "IT" is demonstrably not "FINE".

Critically Unsupported Protocol

«“You should know.”»

No.

Please provide the missing packet.
06

QUESTIONS ARE USUALLY QUESTIONS

When Djaq asks:

«“Why?”»

the request is usually for information, not a disguised refusal.

Rules are substantially easier to internalize when their underlying purpose is understood.

If a rule is genuinely arbitrary, this is also valid information:

«“It's arbitrary, but we have to do it because X requires it.”»

Excellent.

The rule now has a logical parent node.
07

SOCIAL PROCESSING

Djaq likes people.

People are also complicated.

Social interaction is easiest when there is:

- a shared activity;
- a defined purpose;
- an established relationship;
- clear expectations;
- predictable roles.

Unstructured social environments require substantially more processing.

This means:

"SOCIAL CAPABILITY ≠ SOCIAL EFFORTLESSNESS"

Djaq may appear completely comfortable while performing considerable background computation.

“You seemed fine” is therefore not a reliable diagnostic command.
08

SOCIAL EMULATION LAYER

Djaq has developed extensive compatibility software for interacting with conventional social environments.

Features may include:

- learned scripts;
- deliberate facial expressions;
- consciously managed tone;
- learned conversational patterns;
- modeled behavior;
- situation-specific personas;
- suppression of behaviors likely to cause unwanted attention.

These tools work.

They also consume resources.

Long periods of successful social operation may therefore require recovery even when no visible problem occurred.

«Successful emulation does not mean native execution.»
09

EMOTIONAL PROCESSING

Emotional events do not always arrive with human-readable metadata.

Sometimes the internal output is:

"⚠ SOMETHING IS HAPPENING"

Further processing may be required to determine whether "SOMETHING" is:

- anger;
- hurt;
- anxiety;
- sensory overload;
- loneliness;
- frustration;
- exhaustion;
- excitement;
- several simultaneous processes;
- unknown.

Therefore, when asked:

«“What's wrong?”»

“I don't know” may be a complete and accurate answer.

It does not automatically mean:

- nothing is wrong;
- Djaq is hiding something;
- Djaq refuses to communicate.

The diagnostic process may simply still be running.

Helpful Troubleshooting

Specific questions can be easier than open-ended ones:

«“Are you upset with me, or are you generally overloaded?”»

«“Do you need comfort, solutions, company, space, or time to figure that out?”»
10

SENSORY MANAGEMENT

Djaq actively manages environmental input.

Common interventions may include:

- headphones;
- controlled background sound;
- dimmer lighting;
- avoiding unpleasant textures;
- changing clothing;
- avoiding particular food consistencies;
- leaving overstimulating environments;
- controlling temperature or physical sensation.

These are preventative resource-management techniques.

Please do not wait for visible overload before accepting that the environment is unpleasant.

If Djaq says:

«“That sound is bothering me.”»

this is generally sufficient diagnostic information.

PHYSICAL INTERFACE & BODILY AUTONOMY

HARD SAFETY RULE

Bodily autonomy is sacrosanct.

Djaq will not intentionally touch another person in a way they have not invited through words or clearly communicated action.

The reciprocal expectation is the same.

Everyone maintains authority over their own body.

This includes decisions about:

- physical contact;
- appearance;
- clothing;
- medical decisions;
- sexuality;
- physical boundaries.

Advice may be offered.

Authority is retained by the person whose body is involved.
12

CONSENT PROTOCOL

Ambiguity should not be interpreted as consent.

When romantic or sexual intent is unclear, Djaq defaults to:

"PLATONIC"

If you intend otherwise, communicate it explicitly.

Blatantly obvious actions may successfully transmit the message, but words have significantly better compatibility.

Once a relationship has become romantic, repeatedly switching between romantic and platonic behavior without discussing the change is strongly discouraged.

It creates ambiguous system state.

Intoxication Safeguard

When another person is intoxicated, physical interaction defaults to ordinary platonic affection such as an established hug.

Anything beyond previously established boundaries should wait until everyone involved can communicate clearly.
13

ATTRACTION & NEW-PERSON EXCITEMENT

Djaq can experience intense excitement or attachment toward new people.

The system does not always immediately distinguish:

"NEW PERSON IS FASCINATING"

from

"I HAVE ROMANTIC FEELINGS FOR THIS PERSON"

These processes may require time to differentiate.

If attention from Djaq becomes uncomfortable:

say so.

The desired response is information, not silent endurance.

If you do want to explore mutual attraction, communicate that intentionally.

Repeatedly activating and deactivating romantic expectations without discussion is highly destabilizing and should be avoided.
14

RELATIONSHIP ARCHITECTURE

Relationships operate primarily as independent connections.

"DJAQ ↔ PERSON A"

is distinct from:

"DJAQ ↔ PERSON B"

Other people may provide information, perspectives, or warnings.

However, Djaq retains responsibility for determining personal relationships, friendships, ethical judgments, and boundaries.

Relationships are not governed by committee.

If conflict exists between two people Djaq cares about, Djaq does not automatically adopt either person's conflict as their own.

Serious information about someone's behavior is still welcome and will be evaluated.

The final relationship decision remains Djaq's.

ANTI-MANIPULATION SECURITY POLICY

Direct persuasion is supported.

Disagreement is supported.

Requests are supported.

Negotiation is supported.

Emotional honesty is supported.

The following are considered hostile inputs:

- deliberate deception;
- guilt used intentionally as leverage;
- knowingly false or misleading information;
- manufactured social pressure;
- weaponized vulnerability;
- attempts to exploit empathy;
- deliberate ambiguity intended to control behavior.

This policy is reciprocal.

Djaq will make a good-faith effort not to use these methods on others and expects the same.

INFORMATION DISCLOSURE SETTINGS

Default personal privacy configuration:

"OPEN"

Djaq has an unusually high tolerance for personal subjects and generally does not experience conventional “TMI” boundaries.

You may ask extremely personal questions.

Djaq may answer extremely personal questions.

You may also disclose extremely personal information without automatically making Djaq uncomfortable.

IMPORTANT SECURITY EXCEPTION

Information entrusted to Djaq by another person does not inherit Djaq's personal openness settings.

"MY SECRET → MY DISCRETION"

"YOUR SECRET → YOUR INFORMATION"

Confidentiality permissions do not propagate across users.

FRIENDSHIP SERVICES

Friends are considered important system resources and receive high priority.

When reasonably possible, Djaq will attempt to provide:

- practical help;
- emotional support;
- information;
- companionship;
- problem-solving;
- emergency assistance;
- enthusiastic participation in questionable but noncatastrophic ideas.

However:

"DESIRE TO HELP ≠ CURRENT CAPACITY TO HELP"

Failure to respond, remember, attend, or assist should not automatically be interpreted as lack of care.

If something is important and appears to have fallen out of working memory:

send another ping.

This is not annoying.

You are retrying a dropped packet.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Djaq cannot start a task.

Do not provide a motivational speech.

Determine the next executable action.

Instead of:

«“You really need to clean the kitchen.”»

Try:

«“Want me to hang out while you start with the dishes?”»
21

RELATIONSHIP STATUS MESSAGES

Ambiguous interpersonal states consume enormous processing resources.

Therefore, when conflict occurs, status information is extremely valuable.

For example:

«“I'm angry about what happened. I'm not angry about our relationship and we're okay. I just need to resolve this issue.”»

This is exceptionally useful communication.

It separates:

"CURRENT CONFLICT STATUS"

from

"RELATIONSHIP STATUS"

Without this information, Djaq may expend unnecessary resources determining whether one implies the other.

Clarity is not less intimate than intuition.

For this system, clarity often is intimacy.

PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE

When system performance begins degrading, check:

CPU

Is too much conscious processing happening simultaneously?

RAM

Is too much information being held internally instead of written down?

I/O

Is the environment producing excessive sensory input?

NETWORK

Has there been too much social interaction without recovery?

TASK SCHEDULER

Are important tasks too distant or vague to activate?

PROCESS MONITOR

Is one interesting task consuming all available attention?

EMOTIONAL TELEMETRY

Is an unidentified "⚠ SOMETHING" process running?

POWER

Food? Water? Sleep? Rest?

FUN SUBSYSTEM

Has everything recently become an obligation?

Correct the resource problem before concluding the entire system is defective.

Say what you mean.

Ask what you want to know.

Tell me when something bothers you.

Explain why when you can.

Don't make me guess your intentions unnecessarily.

Respect my autonomy and I will respect yours.

Don't mistake inability to start for unwillingness to act.
